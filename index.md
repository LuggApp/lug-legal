# Lug – Local Help

Lug connects you with people nearby for small tasks, things for sale and local gatherings.

## Support

Questions, problems or safety concerns: **support@lug.nz**

We review safety reports and act on violations within 24 hours. If you are in immediate danger, call **111**.
