# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 18 August 2026**

This policy explains what information Lug collects, why, and what we do with it.

Lug is operated by an individual developer based in Auckland, New Zealand. We handle personal information in accordance with the **New Zealand Privacy Act 2020**.

Contact: **luggapp@proton.me**

---

## The short version

- Lug is anonymous. You do not give us your name, email, or phone number.
- We collect your approximate location so the app can show you nearby Lugs.
- We collect a device identifier to stop banned users making new accounts.
- **We keep that device identifier after you delete your account.** This is explained in full below.
- We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

---

## What we collect

### Information you provide

- **Display name** — a name you choose. You do not have to use your real name, and we recommend you do not.
- **Lug content** — the tasks and gatherings you post, including their description, category, payment amount, and location.
- **Messages** — what you send in chat.
- **Ratings** — the stars and tags you give other users.

### Information collected automatically

- **Location.** Lug uses your device's location to show Lugs near you and to place Lugs you post on the map. The location of a Lug you post is visible to other users. We do not track your location in the background or when the app is closed.
- **Device identifier.** We collect Apple's Identifier for Vendor (IDFV) — a value your device provides to us that does not identify you personally and cannot be linked to you outside this app. We use it for one purpose only: to detect when a banned user creates a new account on the same device. See "Device identifiers and bans" below.
- **Push notification token.** If you enable notifications, we store a token so we can send them. This is deleted when you delete your account.
- **Anonymous account identifier.** A randomly generated ID that represents your account. It is not linked to your identity.

### What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment details, or browsing activity. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

## Device identifiers and bans

This section explains something important, so we have set it out clearly.

**What we collect.** Apple's Identifier for Vendor (IDFV). This is a value assigned by your device that is shared only across apps from the same developer. It does not identify you personally, and other companies cannot link it to you.

**Why.** Lug accounts are anonymous, which means a banned user could simply make a new account. Recording the device an account was created on lets us detect that and prevent it.

**How it is used.** When an account is suspended or banned, we record the associated device identifier. If a new account is later created on the same device, it is automatically suspended.

**Retention after account deletion.** When you delete your account, your profile and personal content are removed. However, **if your account was suspended or banned, the associated device identifier is kept** after deletion. If we deleted it along with the account, anyone could evade a ban simply by deleting their account and starting again — which would defeat the purpose of the system entirely.

This retained value is a device identifier only. It is not linked to your name, your content, or any other information about you, and it cannot be used to identify you.

If your account was never suspended or banned, no device identifier is retained after you delete your account.

## Who can see your information

- **Other users** can see your display name, avatar, ratings, and any Lugs or messages you post. The location of a Lug you post is visible on the map.
- **We** can access data stored in the app in order to operate it, investigate reports, and enforce our guidelines.
- **Supabase**, our database and hosting provider, stores this data on our behalf.
- **OpenAI** receives text you post (Lug descriptions, chat messages, display names) for automated moderation screening. It is used only to check the content against safety categories.
- **Expo** handles push notification delivery if you have enabled notifications.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it for advertising.

We may disclose information where we are required to by law, or where we reasonably believe it is necessary to prevent serious harm.

---

## Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers operated by our providers, which may be located outside New Zealand. We take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected to a standard comparable to the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

---

## How long we keep it

- **Your account and content** — until you delete your account.
- **Messages** — messages you sent are anonymised when you delete your account, so conversations remain readable for the other person.
- **Lugs you posted** — reassigned to a placeholder account so other users' history stays intact.
- **Device identifiers of banned accounts** — kept indefinitely, as explained above.
- **Reports** — kept for safety and moderation purposes.

---

## Your account lives on your device

Lug has no login. Your account exists only on the device where you created it.

**If you delete the app, your account cannot be recovered.** Your progress, ratings, and history will be permanently lost. We have no way to restore them, because we have no way to identify you.

## Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** it if it is wrong

Because Lug is anonymous, we may not be able to identify which information relates to you unless you can tell us your account identifier. Contact **luggapp@proton.me** and we will help where we can.

You can delete your account at any time from within the app.

## Children

Lug is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have, we will delete the account.

If you believe a child is using Lug, contact **luggapp@proton.me**.
