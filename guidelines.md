# Community Guidelines

**Last updated: 18 August 2026**

Lug works because people show up, help out, and treat each other decently. These guidelines set out what we expect. They apply to everything on Lug — Lug posts, gatherings, chat messages, display names, and how you behave when you meet someone in person.

Breaking these guidelines can get your content removed and your account suspended or banned.

To report someone or something, use the report option in the app, or email **luggapp@proton.me**.

---

## You must be 18 or older

Lug is for adults only. People meet in person through Lug, so we do not allow anyone under 18 to use it.

If you are under 18, please do not use Lug.

If you think a user is under 18, report them. We take this seriously and will act on it.

---

## Treat people well

**Do not:**

- harass, bully, threaten, or intimidate anyone
- use slurs or attack people for their race, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation
- send unwanted sexual messages, images, or advances
- stalk, follow, or repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop
- share anyone's personal information without their permission

If someone makes you uncomfortable, block them. Blocking stops them contacting you. Reporting tells us about it so we can act.

---

## Keep it legal and safe

**Do not use Lug for:**

- anything illegal
- drugs, alcohol sales, weapons, or stolen goods
- sexual services of any kind
- gambling or money lending
- scams, fraud, phishing, or fake offers
- work that is dangerous, or that requires a licence or certification you do not hold
- anything involving minors

**Do not:**

- ask someone to do something unsafe
- post a Lug you do not intend to honour
- misrepresent what a task involves, or where it is

---

## Be honest

- Describe Lugs accurately — what the task is, where, when, and what you are paying
- Turn up when you say you will, or let the other person know
- Pay what you agreed to pay
- Rate people honestly, based on what actually happened
- Do not use ratings to punish someone for something unrelated

---

## Meeting in person

Lug puts you in touch with strangers. Please look after yourself:

- **Meet in public** where you reasonably can
- **Tell someone** where you are going and when you expect to be back
- **Trust your instincts.** If something feels off, leave. You do not owe anyone an explanation
- **Do not share** your home address, financial details, or identity documents in chat
- **Do not go anywhere** you would not go alone

If you feel unsafe or something happens that worries you, contact us at **luggapp@proton.me**. If you are in immediate danger, call **111**.

---

## Gatherings

Gatherings are group events. The same rules apply, plus:

- do not post gatherings that are unsafe, illegal, or misleading
- respect the spot limit and the host's decisions
- hosts may remove people from a gathering

---

## Reporting and enforcement

You can report any Lug, message, or user from within the app.

When we receive a report, we may:

- remove the content
- warn the user
- suspend or permanently ban the account
- prevent new accounts being created on the same device

We may act without notice where there is a risk to someone's safety.

We use automated moderation to screen text posted on Lug. This is not perfect — it will sometimes miss things and sometimes flag things wrongly. If you think we have got something wrong, email us.

---

## Contact

**luggapp@proton.me**

For safety concerns, please say so in the subject line so we can prioritise it.

If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services on **111**.
